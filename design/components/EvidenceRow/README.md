EvidenceRow is one planned evidence item on the Event screen, built to be ticked with a thumb during an interview.

- **Check box** on the left, 24px inside a `touch-target` row: empty (planned or missing), dash on `warn` (partial or promised), check on `ok` (received).
- **Text**: what was planned, then one muted line with the requirement codes and what is linked or promised.
- **Criticality** under the text.
- **State badge** on the right: Received, Partial, Promised, Missing, or Proposed by AI when a link awaits review.

Tapping the row opens the ItemSheet. A long press, or the row menu on desktop, records a promise: who and by when. Rows are sorted missing first, then by criticality, so the auditor sees what is still open before the interview ends.
