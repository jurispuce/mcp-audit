## What

Lock a finding as a conclusion.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Approve records approved_by, approved_at and approved_version and locks the finding
- [ ] Reopen returns it to draft as a new version
- [ ] Approve all drafts in one action
- [ ] Approval is not available through MCP
