## What

The risk list that drives criticality.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Empty state recommends obtaining the auditee register or recording the auditor view
- [ ] Risk has title, level, origin, many requirements, many scopes
- [ ] Requirements are chosen from a searchable checklist; ticked items stay visible while searching
- [ ] Highest linked risk level is available as the default criticality for a requirement
