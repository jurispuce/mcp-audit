## What

Connect artifacts to planned evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Link an artifact to an evidence item with locator, excerpt and sufficiency (supports, partial, contradicts)
- [ ] Proposed links show accept, reject and accept all
- [ ] Confirming a link sets planned or missing evidence to received
- [ ] One artifact can link to several evidence items
